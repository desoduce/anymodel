Privacy Policy for ChatinShield

Last Updated: January 4, 2026

Important: ChatinShield is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not operate backend servers, do not collect user data, and do not track your activity. All processing happens locally on your device.

Note: For the complete, detailed privacy policy, please see PRIVACY_POLICY.md on GitHub

Summary - What You Need to Know

  • ✅ We don't collect your data
  • ✅ Everything is stored locally on your device
  • ✅ Your API keys are encrypted
  • ✅ No tracking or analytics
  • ✅ You send data directly to LLM providers (not through our servers)
  • ⚠️ LLM providers have their own privacy policies - review them
  • ⚠️ PII filtering helps but isn't perfect - review documents before upload

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No Personal Information
  • No Account Data
  • No Analytics
  • No Location Data
  • No Device Information
  • No Chat History
  • No Documents

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device using encrypted storage:

  • API Keys - Encrypted using device-level encryption
  • App Settings - Your preferences (theme, default provider, etc.)
  • Custom Filter Patterns - User-defined PII filtering rules
  • Processed Documents - Temporarily cached, cleared when you close the app

Third-Party Services

When you use ChatinShield to interact with third-party LLM providers, your data goes directly to them. Please review their privacy policies:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenRouter Privacy Policy

Data Security

  • Encrypted storage for API keys and custom filters
  • System Keychain on iOS for sensitive data
  • No debug logging in production builds
  • File size limits (10MB/file, 50MB total)
  • No hardcoded secrets in application code

Your Data Rights

Since we don't collect data, there's no data to access, modify, or delete from our systems. However, you can:

  • Delete local data: Settings → Clear All Keys
  • Remove all data: Uninstall the app

Children's Privacy

ChatinShield is not directed to children under 13. Recommended age rating: 13+

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy?

  • Email: support@chatinshield.com
  • GitHub: Report an Issue

By using ChatinShield, you consent to this Privacy Policy. Your privacy is our priority.
